# GCI Admin Web Portal - User Manual

Welcome to the Gospel Centres International (GCI) Admin Web Portal user manual. This guide is written to help administrators, pastors, and auditors navigate the portal and execute daily administrative duties.

---

## 1. Accessing the Portal & Dashboard

### Logging In
1. Navigate to the configured web portal URL using your web browser.
2. Input your registered administrative **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **Log In**. Upon verification, you will be redirected to the main dashboard.

---

## 2. Sidebar Menu Items Reference Guide

The sidebar menu allows access to the different administrative modules of GCI. Depending on your user role, some of these menu items may be hidden.

### 2.1. 🏠 Dashboard
- **Functioning**: The central analytics hub of the church administration console.
- **Features**:
  - Displays quick metrics cards for: *Total Registered Members*, *Active Growth Centers*, *Service Collections Sum*, and *Pending Actions*.
  - Displays interactive charts mapping giving histories and trends.
  - Lists notification logs and alerts requiring attention (e.g. pending benevolence claims).

### 2.2. 👥 Members
*Encompasses membership directories.*
- **All Members**:
  - **Functioning**: A searchable registry of all GCI members.
  - **Features**: Allows administrators to view profiles, filter members by assembly, age brackets, or ministry affiliations, and export records to Excel or PDF. Administrators can edit profiles or reset passwords.

### 2.3. ⛪ Church Structure
*Organizes physical assemblies and local cell groups.*
- **Assemblies**:
  - **Functioning**: CRUD portal for GCI assemblies.
  - **Features**: Create new assembly nodes (registering physical address, geographical coordinates, contact details), edit status properties, or toggle active/inactive states. Maps pastors and leadership boards to specific assemblies.
- **Growth Centers**:
  - **Functioning**: Oversees local weekly cell groups.
  - **Features**: Manages cell group registrations, reviews location details, and assigns Cell Leaders to manage weekly attendance.

### 2.4. 🌱 Rapid Church Planting (RCPS)
*Tracks church growth initiatives and seed groups.*
- **Active Rcpss**:
  - **Functioning**: Lists active RCPS teams and regional clusters.
  - **Features**: Setup new cluster groups, track seed group progress, and approve bulk membership relocations.
- **Pledges & Giving**:
  - **Functioning**: Logs financial pledges made towards planting initiatives.
  - **Features**: Register member pledges, track contributions against pledged amounts, and review outstanding pledges.

### 2.5. 👔 Leadership
*Governance management.*
- **Central Leadership**:
  - **Functioning**: Manages the General Executive Council (GEC) members directory.
  - **Features**: Update profiles, terms of service, and photos of central leadership members.
- **Elders**:
  - **Functioning**: Listing of active ordained church elders.
  - **Features**: Assign elders to local assemblies and maintain their contact registers.
- **Deacons**:
  - **Functioning**: Manage profiles and duty assignments for deacons.
  - **Features**: Assign deacons to service rosters and track active duties.

### 2.6. ✝️ Ministries
*Administrative department coordination.*
- **All Ministries**:
  - **Functioning**: Manages the various church departments (e.g. Choir, Media/AV, Ushers, Youth Ministry).
  - **Features**: Create ministries, edit descriptions, assign department heads (Ministry Leaders), and review registered members.

### 2.7. 📅 Events
*Event organization.*
- **Annual Calendar**:
  - **Functioning**: Schedules upcoming events and services.
  - **Features**: Setup paid or free events, upload banners, set coordinates/locations, and outline details for the mobile calendar.
- **Event Registrations**:
  - **Functioning**: Manages booking records.
  - **Features**: Track event bookings, review ticket payments, and review participant feedback ratings.

### 2.8. ⛪ Meetings
*Board and administrative meetings.*
- **Meeting Attendance**:
  - **Functioning**: Logs board and deacon meeting registrations.
  - **Features**: Check historical logs of meetings and verify who was present.
- **Add Meeting**:
  - **Functioning**: Registers upcoming staff and board meetings.
  - **Features**: Input meeting date, agenda, location, and invitee lists.

### 2.9. 📢 Content & Media
*Mobile content delivery.*
- **Announcements**:
  - **Functioning**: Manages banners and announcements pushed to the mobile app home screen.
  - **Features**: Upload announcement graphics, type text contents, and schedule start/end dates for automatic publishing.

### 2.10. 📊 Reports
*Aggregated logs and performance tracking.*
- **All Reports**:
  - **Functioning**: General dashboard summary of all reports.
- **Growth Centers Report**:
  - **Functioning**: Aggregates attendance statistics compiled by cell leaders.
  - **Features**: Track weekly cell attendance rates, identify declining cells, and review new cell visitor registrations.
- **Deacons Report**:
  - **Functioning**: Analyzes weekly observations compiled by Deacons on Duty.
  - **Features**: Read observations on Tuesday Prayers, Thursday Bible Study, Friday Kesha, and Sunday Services. Filters for pastoral care flags.
- **Ministry Leaders Report**:
  - **Functioning**: Consolidates reports submitted by ministry/department heads.

### 2.11. 💵 Finance
*Payments validation.*
- **Payments**:
  - **Functioning**: Portal to audit collections.
  - **Features**: Lists collections submitted by deacons. Displays the drawn digital signatures of compiling deacons to ensure compliance before posting totals.

### 2.12. 🤝 Benevolence
*Welfare cover request manager.*
- **Benevolence Members**:
  - **Functioning**: Central panel for reviewing benevolence applications.
  - **Features**: Audits claims, downloads medical/death certificates, reviews member statuses, enters audit remarks, and transitions claim stages (*Pending -> Under Review -> Approved -> Disbursed*).

### 2.13. 💬 Faith & Fellowship Space
- **Functioning**: Central panel to moderate discussion boards and devotion commentaries.
- **Features**: Monitor devotion comment feeds, edit/remove inappropriate posts, and manage devotion topics.

### 2.14. ⚙️ System Settings
- **Functioning**: System configuration settings.
- **Features**: Real-time management of dynamic API configurations, payment keys, SMS gateway endpoints, T&C document updates, and user account deletion controls.
